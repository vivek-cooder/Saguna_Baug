“A thriving agricultural sector depends on the dignity of farmers, and Agro-tourism is one powerful way to uphold it.”


कृषिभूषण, कृषिरत्न श्री. चंद्रशेखर हरिभाऊ भडसावळे.

Dignity via farming



Saguna Baug is a beautiful, eco-friendly agricultural farm located at the foothills of Matheran, beautifully surrounded by the Ulhas River. Just 2 hours from Mumbai, Pune and Nashik this 60 Acres active farm is Established by Shri Harikaka Bhadsavle in the early 1960s He was a Gandhian freedom fighter, his dream was to build a thriving agricultural farm that would support his family, create local employment and serve as a role model for sustainable rural development.


Today, that dream flourishes under the care of his next generation, They live full-time on the farm and are deeply committed to its original vision and follow a moto “Dignity via farming”. With the help of over 150 dedicated local men and women, Saguna Baug has become a vibrant centre of Agro-Rural tourism and Agricultural research.


Saguna Baug also produces over 400 high-quality, farm-grown and handmade products, ranging from food items to artisanal goods. These are available for purchase offline and online, making it easy for people across the region to enjoy a taste of Saguna Baug wherever they are.


Hari Kaka’s Son कृषिभूषण, कृषिरत्न Mr. Chandrashekhar Bhadsavle is a very important Farmer -Scientist of the country, he has pioneered many nature and farmer friendly concepts such as…


Agro Tourism (कृषी-पर्यटन) – 1985


“शेतकऱ्याने आपल्या फळत्या फुलत्या शेतावरच आयोजित केलेली आनंददायी शिक्षण प्रकिया व मौज यांचा सहज सुंदर मिलाप असलेला उपक्रम म्हणजेच कृषी-पर्यटन.”


Agro-Tourism is defined as, “An activity organized by a farmer on His/her active farm, where leisure, learning, and fun are combined together.”


(Definition of Agro-Tourism by Mr. Chandrashekhar H. Bhadsavle -1990)


SRT (सगुणा रिजनरेटिव्ह तंत्र) – 2011


It is a conservation agriculture, No-till method of farming which does not cause atrocity of tillage, completely stops soil erosion, promotes natural production of earthworms, increases organic carbon of the soil, considerably increases productivity of the land and added effect of amazing happiness & confidence of the farmer.


SVT (सगुणा वनसंवर्धन तंत्र) – 2014


simple and effective technique to stop forest fires and to reproduce green perennial vegetation. Preventing forest fires and thus, restoring the natural vegetation, ultimately enhancing the water infiltration and aquifer recharging capacities is the basic tenant on which the SVT project is built upon.


SJT (सगुणा जलसंवर्धन तंत्र) – 2017


This technique is a comprehensive method aimed at addressing the challenges posed by invasive aquatic weeds, particularly water hyacinth (Eichhornia crassipes) and water lettuce (Pistia stratiotes), which are known for their detrimental effects on aquatic ecosystems.


Aqua Agro-Tourism – 2001


Whether you’re seeking to reconnect with nature, learn about sustainable agriculture, or experience the cultural richness of rural Maharashtra, Saguna Baug welcomes you to discover joy in its soil, stories, and spirit.

Mr. Chandan Bhadsavle, son of Mr. Chandrashekhar Hari Bhadsavle, pursued his education in Hospitality Management from Mumbai & Switzerland. He returned to his ancestral farm in 2006 (Saguna Baug), and has since taken charge of its operations. Under his leadership, the farm has grown remarkably in both reputation and service quality. His visionary approach and dedication to promoting Agro-tourism have played a pivotal role in elevating the farm’s image. In recognition of his outstanding contributions to the field of Agro-tourism, he was honoured with the National Tourism Award in 2018.
