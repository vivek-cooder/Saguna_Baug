Overview

Our Row House rooms offer a spacious and comfortable stay, with four rooms that can each accommodate 4 to 6 people, making them ideal for families or groups. Designed to provide both privacy and togetherness, these rooms blend modern amenities with a cozy atmosphere. The rustic charm of the surroundings combined with the ample space inside ensures a relaxing retreat. Whether you’re enjoying a family getaway or bonding with friends, the Row House rooms provide the perfect setting for a memorable stay at Saguna Baug.


Please note: The Row House is a two-floor structure (Ground + First floor) without a lift, so guests may need to use stairs to access upper-floor rooms.

Organizational Declaration
Saguna Baug is first and foremost a working agricultural farm and an Agro-tourism center – not a conventional hotel or resort. While we strive to ensure a comfortable stay for our guests, we wish to transparently share some inherent characteristics of our rural setting:
  • Our farm features narrow approach roads typical of countryside locations
  • Dining options offer simple, traditional meals rather than extensive menus
  • Occasional interruptions in electricity or water supply may occur
  • Guests may encounter farm animals and insects during their stay
  • Our staff is fluent in Marathi and conversant in Hindi, but other languages may present communication challenges

We embrace these aspects as part of our authentic farm experience, and appreciate your understanding as we share the genuine rhythms of agricultural life.Please go through our Policies for more details.
