Overview

A perfect getaway for families, friends, school trips, corporates, and solo travellers alike.

Spend a refreshing day at Saguna Baug, where tourists meet rural culture and enjoy life on a farm. Our one-day picnic package offers a delightful blend of leisure-learning and adventure. Whether you’re looking to explore sustainable farming, interact with farm animals, or simply unwind by the pond with fresh, home-cooked meals, this experience has something for everyone. It’s a great opportunity to bond with loved ones and experience the simplicity and richness of rural life.

Come with your group, and return home with lasting memories!

Organizational Declaration
Saguna Baug is first and foremost a working agricultural farm and an Agro-tourism center – not a conventional hotel or resort. While we strive to ensure a comfortable stay for our guests, we wish to transparently share some inherent characteristics of our rural setting:
  • Our farm features narrow approach roads typical of countryside locations
  • Dining options offer simple, traditional meals rather than extensive menus
  • Occasional interruptions in electricity or water supply may occur
  • Guests may encounter farm animals and insects during their stay
  • Our staff is fluent in Marathi and conversant in Hindi, but other languages may present communication challenges

We embrace these aspects as part of our authentic farm experience, and appreciate your understanding as we share the genuine rhythms of agricultural life.Please go through our Policies for more details.

Disclaimer: Information presented on our website, social media platforms, brochures, signage, or any other promotional material is subject to change. Certain activities or features may not be available on the day of your visit to Saguna Baug due to maintenance, seasonal variations, or operational considerations. We appreciate your understanding.
